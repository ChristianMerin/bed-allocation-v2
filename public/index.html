How the setup works

  1. 1
    Wards are specialties (Medical, Orthopedic, Pediatric…). Add them here.
  2. 2
    Rooms belong to a ward and have a class: Private (1 bed), Semi-Private (2 beds) or Ward (up to 12 beds). Add them in Rooms.
  3. 3
    Prices come from Room Rates. Any single room can still get a special price.

Who can sign in to this system

Admin: everything, including wards, rooms, room rates and users. Staff (nurses, admitting clerks): bed board, admit / transfer / discharge, bed status and reservations. New users get a temporary password and must change it at first sign-in.
